how long will my money last?

A student photographs a statement. Code, not the model, says how the month compares with what is left after rent.

Pink Pawika · October 26, 2026

The problem

After rent, how far does the living allowance go?

Who

An international student whose I-20 already states a yearly living figure. Rent takes a known share of it.

Today

They type a photo into a spreadsheet, Copilot, or Monarch. A chatbot can read one file, then the answer stays in the chat.

Cost

Entering one statement by hand takes about 30 minutes. A bad minus sign is hard to spot later.

The miss

Bank apps list charges. They do not answer what is left after rent.

What is new

Built for one living allowance, not a bank feed.

Mint, Copilot Money, Monarch Money, Expensify, and ChatGPT with a file upload already exist. They stop before this ledger.

  • The model only reads. Code does the math, in integer cents.
  • A receipt with a total is one store row. A statement is one row per line. Balances are not purchases.
  • Green amounts are money received and are left out of the list.
  • If a printed total does not match the lines, every line waits in review.
  • The I-20 path uses yearly living expenses only. Nothing is saved until rent is saved.

How the agents work

A pipeline. The model has no tools of its own.

Safety

A document can be read. It cannot give orders.

  • Untrusted text is prefaced and redacted before the model sees it.
  • The extract schema has no category, so a file cannot set Income.
  • A hostile $4.50 sample stays 450 cents, category Other, status review.
  • The live gateway blocked that file. The proof is the unit test.
  • The day stops at 40 runs. A retry is the same run, not a second one.
